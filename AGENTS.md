## Scope

This repository is a curated awesome-list of open source **payments, banking, compliance, and financial-infrastructure** tools.

Do **not** add or accept:

- Trading systems, bots, or strategies
- Technical analysis indicators or backtesting
- Candlestick / OHLC / market charts
- Stock quotes, market data, or equities APIs
- Quarterly reports, SEC filings, fundamentals, or similar investor-research tools

Reject those pull requests. Point contributors at quant/trading lists instead.
